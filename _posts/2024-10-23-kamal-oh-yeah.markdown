We own very little anymore... even our words are falling into the hands of others. I want to push back against this.

When my business works with a client, an enormous part of the value proposition is that they own what we build for them. This is only part of the battle in software though. There is an increasingly prevalent trend in software to offload all dev ops to big venture backed groups. Sounds attractive until it isn't. Enter Kamal.

Kamal allows small teams to manage dev ops again. DHH introduces it well here: [https://world.hey.com/dhh/kamal-2-thou-need-not-paas-c9e8bd53](https://world.hey.com/dhh/kamal-2-thou-need-not-paas-c9e8bd53
