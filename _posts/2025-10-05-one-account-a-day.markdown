### The Problem

My online footprint is abysmal... more than 100 accounts opened over the years. Most of which are unneccessary. Reflecting on this gives me an almost dirty feeling with so many questions. Do I have a credit card on file with them? Is their site's security solid? The list goes on.

### The Solution

I am going to delete one account a day. I just started. It was the "Ghost CMS" account that I do not use. Nothing against them, but it is time to be intentional with my digital life and that means deleting 99% of the accounts I don't use. Will they honor my account deletion request and actually delete the data? Some will, some won't. I am going to do my part though.
